About Us

We are a small sports fishing charter based in kaafu atoll Maldives .we take you to fishing to the most famous reefs in kaafu atoll Maldives.

Our boat is fully equipped with all necessary things including every items for Popping and Jigging. If you a beginner or expert our crew will assist you with the techniques and will make sure you have a wonderful trip.

Meeting Point

  • Location: Hulhumalé Lakudi Jetty
  • Time: Please arrive 20 minutes before departure
  • What to bring: Sunscreen, a hat, and your ID/Passport
