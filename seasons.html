Fishing Seasons

All year around fishing in kaafu atoll is good but sometimes the ocean changes in the monsoon.

Northeast Monsoon (Nov – Apr)

The sea is normally calm and this is the best time for fishing sailfish and yellowfin tuna.

Southwest Monsoon (May – Oct)

The weather becomes rough but popping is good. you can expect Yellowfin tuna and wahoo.
